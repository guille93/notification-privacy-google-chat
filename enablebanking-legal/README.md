# enablebanking-legal

Public legal pages (privacy policy and terms of service) for the personal,
non-commercial Enable Banking application "HomePi Finance" (BankingSync +
self-hosted Actual Budget), required by the Enable Banking Control Panel
when registering a Production application.

- Privacy Policy: `privacy.html`
- Terms of Service: `terms.html`

Served publicly via jsDelivr, e.g.
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/guille93/notification-privacy-google-chat@main/enablebanking-legal/privacy.html`
