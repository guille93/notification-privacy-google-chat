Last updated: 2026-11-07

1. Controller

Guillermo Cordero Moreno (“the controller”). Contact: guille93@users.noreply.github.com

2. What this application is

“HomePi Finance” is a personal, non-commercial open-banking client (BankingSync) that reads account information and transactions (AIS) of the controller’s own bank accounts via Enable Banking Oy (a licensed TPP) under PSD2, and writes them into a self-hosted Actual Budget instance for personal budgeting.

3. Data processed

  • Account metadata (account name, IBAN as provided by the bank, balance) and transactions of the controller’s own accounts at Santander and Revolut.
  • Technical logs of the local services (no bank data shared with third parties).

4. Purpose and legal basis

Personal budgeting and financial overview. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by the controller when authorizing each bank account. Consent can be withdrawn at any time at the bank, in Enable Banking, or by deleting the local data.

5. Storage and location

Data is stored on the controller’s self-hosted server located in Spain. The services are reachable only through a private Tailscale tailnet with per-device identity checks; on the LAN they are bound to 127.0.0.1 only and are not publicly exposed. Backups are encrypted with age; the private key exists only on the controller’s personal computer.

6. Recipients and sharing

The only recipients are (a) the controller’s own banks, for authentication/consent, and (b) Enable Banking Oy, as the licensed third-party provider relaying the bank data under PSD2. Data is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used for profiling. No third-party analytics or trackers are used.

7. Retention

Bank data is kept while the corresponding bank consent is active; encrypted backups are retained for 14 days and then deleted.

8. Security measures

TLS in transit, tailnet-only access, least-privilege containers (no root, read-only filesystems, dropped capabilities), and encrypted backups.

9. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent, per GDPR. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). Contact: guille93@users.noreply.github.com
